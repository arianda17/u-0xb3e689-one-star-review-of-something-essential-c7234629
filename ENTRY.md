## Why the winner fits the judge criteria

1. Everyday essential, deadpan, unmistakably about sleep itself — a real complaint filed in earnest against something that cannot be fixed.
2. Sharpest true grievance: a full night's sleep that still leaves you tired is universal and never written down as a complaint.
3. Deadpan held throughout: one star, no winking, no explaining the joke, no exclamation marks doing the work.
4. Turn lands at the very end: "worst return policy I have ever used" delivers the sting after the complaint has been read straight.
5. Tight and specific: one joke told cleanly, 94 characters, no filler and no second joke crowding the first.
